# M4.1 application model and the applicant's flow

The offline tests prove the rules ([test_applications.lua](../../Tests/test_applications.lua), [test_ui_apply.lua](../../Tests/test_ui_apply.lua)). In M4.1 the application is **only saved on your character**: nothing is sent to the guild (that is M4.2), so the states *Received*, *Accepted* and *Declined* cannot be reached in game yet. Compare with [mockup 01](../specs/mockups/img/clean/01-browse.webp) (the card), [mockup 02](../specs/mockups/img/clean/02-guild-page.webp) (the guild page) and [mockup 06 D](../specs/mockups/img/clean/06-states.webp) (My Application).

## What you need

- A character **without a guild** (D) on WoW Forever with a **development build** (`tools/install_addon.py`), ideally a class with several roles (a Druid, a Paladin or a Shaman) and a second character of another class on the same account.
- Two or three guilds in Find a Guild: real listings, or `/gf debug seed` (fake listings that exist only on your computer; they are enough here because nothing is sent). At least one should ask for a level above yours, and one should have applications closed (a Guild Master turns *Accept applications through the addon* off in the editor).
- `/console scriptErrors 1`.

## Steps

### The button

- [ ] D: `/gf`, Find a Guild. Every card of a guild that accepts applications has a red **Apply** at the bottom right of its upper part, over the banner. Hover it: the tooltip *Apply to Aurora* and a line about what is sent. **Look**: compare with mockup 01; the button does not cover the tags or the name.
- [ ] A guild whose minimum level is above yours: the button is grey and says **Requirements not met**; the tooltip says which (*Requirements not met: level 40*). A click does nothing.
- [ ] A guild with applications closed: **no button** on its card; its page says *Applications via addon are closed. Use the contacts below.* and has no button either.
- [ ] Open a guild page: **Apply to *Guild*** is under the tags, as wide as the left page, with a line under it. Nothing overlaps the contacts; the page fits at 60 % and 140 % (`/gf scale 60`, `/gf scale 140`).

### The confirmation

- [ ] Press Apply: a box over the window asks *Apply to Aurora with Name (Class level)?* with your real name, class and level. **Record** whether they are right.
- [ ] The roles offered are only those of your class (Warrior: Tank, Melee DPS; Mage, Hunter, Warlock: Ranged DPS; Rogue: Melee DPS; Priest: Healer, Ranged DPS; Paladin: Tank, Healer, Melee DPS; Shaman: Healer, Melee DPS, Ranged DPS; Druid: all four). None is ticked. Tick one, untick it.
- [ ] **Cancel**: the box goes, nothing changed. **Esc** with the box open closes the window; open it again: the box is gone.
- [ ] Press Apply again, tick a role, **Apply**: the note *Saved. It will be delivered when an officer of Aurora comes online.* with a **View** button. **Look**: the box matches the window's style (parchment, the gold edge).

### After applying

- [ ] The card now says **Applied · Waiting for an officer** (parchment, not grey); a click opens My Application. Every other card says **You already applied to Aurora**, grey; its tooltip says *Withdraw it first: one open application at a time.* A long guild name is cut on the button and complete in the tooltip.
- [ ] My Application: the guild's emblem and name, *Applied just now*, the card *Waiting for an officer to come online* with its explanation, the road *SENT > RECEIVED > ACCEPTED / DECLINED* with SENT lit, the role you ticked, *Expires in 14 days without a decision.*, **View guild** and **Withdraw**. The left page has your character (*Name · Class level*) and *Want another guild? Withdraw first...*. **Look**: compare with mockup 06 D.
- [ ] **View guild** opens the guild page on the My Application tab; **Back** returns.
- [ ] `/reload`: the application is still there, on the cards and in My Application.
- [ ] Log in your **second character** (no guild): it has no application, its Apply buttons are red, its confirmation offers the roles of **its** class. Log back in the first: the application is still there.
- [ ] **Withdraw** asks first; **Cancel** keeps it. Withdraw for real: the note *Application withdrawn...*, the card says *Withdrawing...*, Withdraw is gone, and every card says **Apply** again (the same guild too).
- [ ] `/gf debug`, section **Applications**: the number of records, the open one's state, how many are waiting to be sent. No names.
- [ ] No Lua error appeared during any of the above.

### What only time can show (optional)

- [ ] Leave an application open and come back after 14 days (or change nothing and read `/gf debug` the next days): at the login after the 14th day a chat line says *Your application to Aurora expired...*, a dot (a "1") is on the My Application tab until you open it, and the tab says *Expired*.

## What to report if it does not work

| Symptom | What it means | What to send |
|---|---|---|
| the question shows a wrong name, class or level, or "?" | the client functions the addon reads (`UnitName`, `UnitClass`, `UnitLevel`) answer differently on Forever | `/gf debug` Compat section and what the question says |
| the roles offered do not match your class | the class id of `UnitClass` is not the one of SPEC-03 §5 | your class and the roles offered |
| the application is gone after `/reload`, or another character sees it | the per-character saved data is not written or not read | `/gf debug` Saved data and Applications sections |
| every button says *Your character can't send addon messages yet* | the channel refused the addon's messages (listen-only mode, SPEC-01 §8) | `/gf debug` Transport section |
| the box, the button or the tab overlaps or runs out of the window | a layout size is wrong | a screenshot and the window scale |

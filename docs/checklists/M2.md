# M2 exit check: a test listing announced by one client appears in another

The simulator shows that the catalog sync converges inside the traffic budget ([test_sync_sim.lua](../../Tests/test_sync_sim.lua)). This check shows that WoW Forever carries it. It is part of the **exit criterion of milestone M2** and it is **not done yet**. Record the result in the table at the end and tick the box in [M2-find-a-guild.md](../roadmap/M2-find-a-guild.md).

It needs the M1 check first ([M1.md](M1.md)): if two clients cannot exchange a ping, they cannot exchange listings either.

## What you need

- Two clients on WoW Forever at the same time, on the **same realm and faction** (listings are kept per realm and faction).
- A **development build** on both: a copy of the repository installed with `tools/install_addon.py`. In a development build `Config.DEV` is `true`, because the BigWigs packager comments out the `--@debug@` block of `Core/Config.lua` only when it builds a release. A packaged release ignores test listings and refuses `/gf debug announce`.

## Steps

1. Log in on both clients and wait about 30 seconds (the channel is joined after 5 s, the first catalog request goes 5–15 s later).
2. On both clients type `/gf debug`. In the **Sync** section expect `development build`, and after the first sync `last full sync 0 min ago` (or `last: gave up (no answer)` when no other client was online yet: that is fine).
3. On client A type `/gf debug announce`. Expect `Test listing 'AGF Test <name>' announced on the channel.`
4. On client B, within a few seconds, `/gf debug` shows `ANN 1` in the **Sync** section and one more listing in the **Catalog** section (`Listings: 1 listed`). When the Find a Guild list exists (M2.5) the guild `AGF Test <name of A>` appears in it.
5. Log out client B, wait one minute, log in again more than 10 minutes after its last sync (or delete `syncAt` from its SavedVariables): its catalog request is answered by A, and `/gf debug` shows `received 1 summaries` (`CAT` by whisper).
6. Optional, with a release build on a third client: it never shows the test listing (`test listings ignored 1`).

Do not announce a test listing from a character that announces a real guild: a sender that announces two guilds within 24 hours is ignored by the other clients for a day (INT-04).

## What to report if it does not work

| Symptom | What it means | What to send |
|---|---|---|
| `only works in development builds` | the installed copy is a packaged release | how the addon was installed |
| `ANN 0` on B, `Transport: normal` on both | the announcement did not arrive | `/gf debug` from both clients |
| `ANN 0`, `rejected 1` on B | the listing was refused (realm or faction differ, or INT-03/INT-04) | `/gf debug` from B, both realms and factions |
| `Transport: listen-only` | the channel refuses addon messages; requests go by whisper | `/gf debug` from both clients |
| step 5: `requests 4 (... retries 3)` and nothing received | nobody answered the catalog request | `/gf debug` from both clients |

`/gf debug` never prints player or guild names except the test listing's name in the announce message.

## Result

| Date | Clients | Same realm/faction | ANN A → B | CAT A → B (step 5) | Release ignores it | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

# M2.4 look and feel: main window and widgets

The offline tests prove what the window does ([test_ui_nav.lua](../../Tests/test_ui_nav.lua), [test_ui_widgets.lua](../../Tests/test_ui_widgets.lua), [test_ui_window.lua](../../Tests/test_ui_window.lua)). Nobody has seen it in the game yet, so this list is **not done**. Tick each box in [M2-find-a-guild.md](../roadmap/M2-find-a-guild.md) (step M2.4) when it holds, and write what is wrong in the table at the end. Use a development build (`tools/install_addon.py`), one client is enough.

## Opening

- [ ] `/gf` opens the window and `/gf` again closes it; `/guildfinder` does the same.
- [ ] The minimap button exists; a left click opens and closes the window, a right click opens it on **Settings**; its tooltip lists the two clicks.
- [ ] The addon appears in the Addon Compartment menu; clicking it opens the window; hovering shows the tooltip.
- [ ] *Key Bindings → AddOns* has **Toggle Guild Finder**; the key you assign opens and closes the window.
- [ ] **Esc** closes the window; the round gold button at the top right corner closes it too; both work after you used the search or any field (Esc first leaves the field).
- [ ] The window opens again at the same place after `/reload` and after logging out.

## Tabs of the role

Check with the characters you have: a character without a guild, a member, an officer and the Guild Master (the matrix is in [SPEC-00 §5](../specs/SPEC-00-vision-scope.md#5-ruoli-e-pannelli-visibili)).

- [ ] **Settings** is the first tab for every role, at the top of the rail, in the same place.
- [ ] No guild: Settings, Find a Guild, My Application. Member: Settings, My Guild. Officer: Settings, My Guild. Guild Master: Settings, Create Listing.
- [ ] The tabs are one under the other with no gaps; the active one is gold, wider and sticks out; each has an icon and a readable label (two lines for My Application).
- [ ] Leaving the guild or joining one with the window **open** rebuilds the rail and returns to the first tab of the new role.

## Look (use `/gf debug gallery`, development builds)

- [ ] The gallery page opens, with the title "Widget gallery (development)", and Back returns to the previous tab page.
- [ ] Buttons look like glossy pills: red (primary), blue (Discord), parchment (tonal), no fill (ghost), grey (disabled, with the reason written next to it). Hover brightens them and a click darkens them.
- [ ] Chips show a check mark when on; the group allows three at most; the switches slide and turn green; the segmented control shows the selection in brown.
- [ ] The search field has a metal ring that turns gold when it has focus, a placeholder that disappears when you type, and a clear button.
- [ ] The copy field selects its whole text when you click it or press its button, undoes anything you type, and a snackbar says to press Ctrl+C.
- [ ] Class icons show the right icon with a ring in the class colour (WARRIOR … DRUID) at 20, 26 and 40 px; the role badges show four different symbols (shield, cross, swords, target) and their tooltips give the names.
- [ ] Tags, badges ("99+" above 99), the three card kinds (plain, raised, gold) and the plates match [the design system mockup](../specs/mockups/img/clean/00-design-system.webp).
- [ ] The snackbar appears at the bottom of the window and goes away by itself.
- [ ] If a class icon is a green square: the texture `Interface\Icons\ClassIcon_<Class>` does not exist on Forever and `Data/Assets.lua` must use the atlas `classicon-<class>` (or the reverse); record which one works.

## Fonts

- [ ] `/gf debug` shows the **Interface** section with `Fonts: ... on the client's font` if a file of `Media/Fonts/` cannot be loaded; with the bundled Roboto files it must show `... styles from Media/Fonts, 0 on the client's font` and the text must be Roboto (Roboto Mono for the mono line of the gallery).
- [ ] No text is cut off or overlaps another at any scale below.

## Scale and position

- [ ] `/gf scale 60`, `/gf scale 100` and `/gf scale 140` (and the size control of the Settings tab) resize the whole window; text stays readable at 60 % and 140 %, nothing is cut off, the rail and the close button stay on the screen.
- [ ] On a small window of the game (low resolution, high UI scale), 140 % is reduced so that the book, the rail and the close button fit; `/gf scale` still reports 140 %.
- [ ] The window keeps its place on the screen when the scale changes.
- [ ] Dragging by the left page and by the title bar moves the window; it cannot be dragged off the screen; `/gf reset` puts it back in the middle.
- [ ] Backspace goes back on a page that has a Back button (from M2.7) and does nothing in a field.

# M2.6 look and feel and behaviour: the filters

The offline tests prove the rules ([test_filter_model.lua](../../Tests/test_filter_model.lua)) and the controls on the mock client ([test_ui_filters.lua](../../Tests/test_ui_filters.lua)). Nobody has used the filters in the game yet, so this list is **not done**. Tick each box in [M2-find-a-guild.md](../roadmap/M2-find-a-guild.md) (step M2.6) when it holds. Use a development build and `/gf debug seed 60` (or `500`) so there is something to filter; the seeded guilds are local and cover the three commitments, several languages, activities, days and times.

## First opening and the plate

- [ ] A character that never used the filters opens Find a Guild with only **Recruiting only** on; the plate reads **Filters · 1 active** and the guilds that are not recruiting are not in the list (the count reads *N of M guilds*).
- [ ] Every control of the left page is visible without scrolling and nothing overlaps or is cut off at 60 %, 100 % and 140 % (`/gf scale`): four switches, My role, Guild language, Commitment, Activity, Play days and time, Min. active members. The look is close to [the mockup](../specs/mockups/img/clean/01-browse.webp).
- [ ] Switching a filter on or off changes the plate (*Filters · 2 active* ...) and the list within a moment; the list goes back to its top.
- [ ] The round **×** button on the plate turns every filter off (also Recruiting only) and the list shows all guilds; its tooltip says *Reset all filters*.

## Each filter (compare with what the guild cards show)

- [ ] **Looking for my class**: only guilds that show your class icon, or *Looking for any class*, remain; the label names your class.
- [ ] **Requirements met**: guilds with a red *Requirements not met* tag disappear.
- [ ] **New player friendly**: fewer guilds; open a seeded one in the debug output if in doubt.
- [ ] **My role**: the chips show the role icons; roles your class cannot play are dimmed, a click does nothing and the tooltip says why; picking a role keeps guilds that look for it for your class or for any class.
- [ ] **Guild language**: the list opens under the field and stays open while you tick languages; the field shows the names, then *3 selected*; a click outside closes it; the cards keep a language you picked among their tags.
- [ ] **Commitment**: the chips narrow the list to the guilds with that tag; hovering a chip explains the level.
- [ ] **Activity**: the list scrolls with the mouse wheel (13 entries); the guilds that remain list one of the picked activities.
- [ ] **Play days**: the seven squares toggle; only guilds whose *days* line contains one of them remain. **Check with your real time zone**: pick the day a card shows after the conversion.
- [ ] **Play time**: *From* and *To* open a list of times every 30 minutes (opening near 20:00) that scrolls to 23:30; with *20:00* to *23:30* the guilds whose time line overlaps remain; a guild whose line says *All day* always remains; *Any* clears the bound. - [ ] **Min. active members**: the list opens and the guilds with fewer active characters than the number disappear.
- [ ] A list that is open closes when you switch tab, close the window or click anywhere else, and never stays on the screen on its own.

- [ ] Several filters together keep only the guilds that pass all of them; the search box narrows the same list; clearing the search keeps the filters.
- [ ] When nothing matches, the page says *No guild matches your filters* and **Reset filters** turns everything off, the controls too.
- [ ] After `/reload` and after logging out and in, every control, the plate and the list are as you left them. A second character of yours has its own filters (first time: only Recruiting only).
- [ ] No Lua error appears while using any of the above (`/console scriptErrors 1`).

# M2.7 look and feel and behaviour: the guild page

## Left page

- [ ] Emblem placeholder (150 px, metal frame, gold shield), the name in big bold type (two lines at most), the subtitle, then the tags: languages (main first), **HORDE** (red) or **ALLIANCE**, the commitment (green, amber or red), and **May be inactive** (amber) when it applies. - [ ] No **Apply** button yet. A guild that closed applications shows *Applications via addon are closed. Use the contacts below.*

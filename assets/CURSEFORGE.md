# CurseForge gallery: the images to upload

You do **not** need all 80 images of `assets/`. These 12 explain the whole addon; upload them **in this order** (the first is the cover). The title is at most 50 characters and the description at most 160, to stay well under CurseForge's limits (check the limit shown in the upload form if it is stricter). Files are WebP: if the form does not accept WebP, convert them to PNG or JPG (e.g. `python -c "from PIL import Image; Image.open('x.webp').save('x.png')"`).

All are **design previews, not game screenshots**: each says so in its bottom strip, and every description ends with "Design preview". Replace them with real screenshots after the first in-game test.

## 1. `find-a-guild-filters.webp`

- **Title** (32): Find a Guild: search and filters
- **Description** (142): Browse the guilds of your realm and faction, A-Z. Filter by class wanted, role, language, commitment, activity and play times. Design preview.

## 2. `guild-page-recruiting.webp`

- **Title** (14): A guild's page
- **Description** (143): Who the guild is, the classes and roles it needs, activity, play times, member statistics and requirements. One click to apply. Design preview.

## 3. `guild-page-requirements-unmet.webp`

- **Title** (35): Requirements shown before you apply
- **Description** (101): If you do not meet the level or item level, Apply is greyed out and says exactly why. Design preview.

## 4. `apply-confirmation.webp`

- **Title** (18): Apply in one click
- **Description** (136): Confirm your character and the roles you play. Only name, class, race and level go to the officers: no form, no message. Design preview.

## 5. `my-application-received.webp`

- **Title** (23): Follow your application
- **Description** (104): See whether it was sent, received, accepted or declined. One open application at a time. Design preview.

## 6. `editor-activity.webp`

- **Title** (33): Guild Masters: build your listing
- **Description** (119): A guided editor in seven steps: identity, about, activity, recruitment, requirements, contact, publish. Design preview.

## 7. `editor-recruitment.webp`

- **Title** (36): Say which classes and roles you need

## 8. `emblem-designer.webp`

## 9. `applications-pending.webp`
